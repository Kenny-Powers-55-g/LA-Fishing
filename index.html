    Scores are estimates, not guarantees.

    One number per spot, per fish

    Each spot gets a score from 0 to 100 for each kind of fish you chase. It's built from the water temperature, wind, tide and moon, and it's updated every hour.

    A higher number means the conditions match what that fish likes right now. Scores are estimates, not guarantees.
