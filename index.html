How it works

  1. Choose your fish. Pick the species you target, or start with a few common ones.
  2. See the scores. Every spot is ranked from current water, wind, tide and moon readings.
  3. Go fishing. Head for the spot that's turning on, then log your catch to learn what works for you.

How the score is built

A score from 0 to 100 for each spot and each fish. Higher means the conditions match what that fish likes right now.

Poor 0–34Fair 35–54Good 55–74Excellent 75–100
Water
Temperature, clarity and salinity, compared with what each fish prefers.
Wind
Speed and direction, and how they change through the day.
Tide
Water movement and level, where the data allows.
Moon
Moon phase and light, which affect when fish feed.

Scores are estimates from public data. They are not guarantees that you'll catch fish.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can use the map and see every score without an account.

How accurate are the scores?

They're estimates built from public readings and simple rules. Use them to decide where to start, not as a promise. Your own log will show what works for you.

Is my information private?

Your catches and saved spots stay with you. Friends only see what you choose to share with them. See the Privacy page for details.

Can I use it for navigation or safety?

No. OceanCast is not for navigation or safety decisions. Check official forecasts and use your own judgment on the water.
